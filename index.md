## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I select all apps at once?
On Home, long-press the multiselect button to select or deselect every app in the current tag. On the Apps screen, use **Select all** to select the displayed apps.

### Can Hail freeze itself by accident?
No. Hail is protected from freeze and unfreeze operations, including Select All, API calls, and bulk actions.

### How do I see system apps?
Open **Apps** and choose the **All** filter. You can also filter by frozen or unfrozen state.

### Where is the About page?
Open **Settings** and select the information icon. The bottom navigation and navigation rail are hidden while About is open.

### What are Actions?
Actions unfreeze selected apps and then launch another app. Create and manage them from the **Actions** tab, or pin an action to the home screen.

### How do I backup or restore Hail data?
Open **Settings**, then choose **Backup** or **Restore**. Select the data categories to include and choose a ZIP file.

### Why does an app list look stale?
Pull to refresh after installing, removing, freezing, or unfreezing apps. If metadata or icons are still stale, use **Settings > Cache > Clear and rebuild cache**.
